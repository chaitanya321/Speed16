Session-Wise Curriculum

48 live sessions of 90 minutes each. The detailed sequence below follows the updated Skill Booster Program curriculum.

Skill Cluster 1 — THINK SMART

Sessions 1–10 • 15 Hours
SESSION 01

Aptitude & Quantitative Reasoning — Foundations

Numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, estimation, mental calculation and speed calculation strategies. Activity: mental mathematics, estimation and shopping/budget challenges.

SESSION 02

Arithmetic, Percentages & Data-Based Reasoning

Percentage increase/decrease, profit/loss, simple interest, averages, ratio/proportion, unitary method, mixtures and interpreting numerical information.

SESSION 03

Logical Reasoning

Statements and conclusions, patterns and sequences, number/alphabet patterns, coding-decoding, classification and analogies.

SESSION 04

Puzzles & Brain Challenges

Sudoku, logical grids, seating arrangements, directions, blood relations, clocks/calendars, matching and constraint-based puzzles.

SESSION 05

Problem Solving Fundamentals

Problem identification, decomposition, constraints, assumptions and root-cause analysis using 5W1H, 5 Whys, problem trees and cause-and-effect diagrams.

SESSION 06

Critical & Analytical Thinking

Facts vs opinions, assumptions, evidence, cause vs correlation, bias, logical fallacies, argument evaluation and evidence-based decision-making.

SESSION 07

Creative Thinking

Creativity vs intelligence, divergent/convergent thinking, brainstorming, SCAMPER, Six Thinking Hats, mind mapping and lateral thinking.

SESSION 08

Design Thinking

Empathize, define, ideate, prototype and test; user persona, empathy map, problem statement, How Might We questions and user journey.

SESSION 09

Innovation & Ideation Challenge

Innovation vs invention, incremental vs disruptive innovation, unmet needs, opportunity identification, idea evaluation, feasibility, desirability and viability.

SESSION 10

Think Smart Challenge

Integrated aptitude, logical, critical, creative and design-thinking challenges. Mini-project: identify a real-world problem and propose an innovative solution.

Skill Cluster 2 — WORK SMART

Sessions 11–25 • 22.5 Hours
SESSION 11

Digital Literacy Fundamentals

Digital devices, operating systems, files/folders, file formats, cloud computing, browsers, search engines, uploads/downloads, digital identity and troubleshooting.

SESSION 12

Internet & Online Communication

Internet fundamentals, websites/web apps, URLs/domains, email, professional communication, email etiquette, CC/BCC, attachments and video conferencing.

SESSION 13

Data Literacy Fundamentals

Data types, structured/unstructured data, collection, quality, cleaning, interpretation, mean, median, mode, percentages and ratios.

SESSION 14

Excel / Spreadsheet Fundamentals

Workbooks, worksheets, rows/columns, cell references, formatting, SUM, AVERAGE, MIN/MAX, COUNT and relative/absolute references. Practical: student marksheet.

SESSION 15

Excel for Data Analysis

Sorting, filtering, conditional formatting, IF, COUNTIF, SUMIF, data validation, tables and basic lookup concepts.

SESSION 16

Excel Charts & Dashboards

Bar/column/pie/line charts, choosing charts, data storytelling, dashboard design and KPIs. Project: Student Performance Dashboard.

SESSION 17

AI Literacy

Artificial Intelligence, AI vs ML vs DL, Generative AI, Large Language Models, Computer Vision, NLP, recommendation systems and AI applications.

SESSION 18

Generative AI

Text, image and code generation, summarization, translation, content creation, AI assistants and limitations of GenAI.

SESSION 19

Prompt Engineering — Fundamentals

Prompt structure using Role + Context + Task + Constraints + Output Format; examples and conversion of poor prompts into effective prompts.

SESSION 20

Advanced Prompt Engineering

Zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought concept, step-by-step task decomposition, structured output, prompt refinement, evaluation and iterative prompting.

SESSION 21

AI Productivity

AI for study planning, notes, presentations, emails, brainstorming, coding assistance, research, summarization, translation and personal productivity.

SESSION 22

Digital Workspace Tools

Cloud documents, spreadsheets, presentations, forms, cloud drives, calendar, collaboration, file sharing and version history (GIT). Practical: shared project workspace.

SESSION 23

Digital Safety & Cybersecurity Awareness

Password security, MFA, phishing, social engineering, malware, ransomware, safe browsing, public Wi-Fi, device security and data privacy.

SESSION 24

Research & Information Literacy

Research questions, reliable sources, primary/secondary sources, Google Scholar, digital libraries, citation, plagiarism, copyright, fact checking and AI-generated information verification.

SESSION 25

Research Writing, Publishing & Personal Productivity

Research paper structure, references, ethics, book/article writing basics, time management, goal setting, Eisenhower Matrix, Pomodoro and weekly planning. Practical: six-page mini research proposal and 100-page book project.

Skill Cluster 3 — CREATE & BUILD

Sessions 26–40 • 22.5 Hours
SESSION 26

Computational Thinking

Decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction, algorithm design and logical thinking. Activities include converting everyday activities into algorithms.

SESSION 27

Algorithms & Flowcharts

Algorithms, pseudocode, flowcharts, sequence, decisions and iteration. Design flowcharts for ATM, login, student grading and shopping systems.

SESSION 28

Web Development Fundamentals

How the web works, client/server, website vs web application, HTML, CSS, JavaScript overview, browsers, URLs and domains.

SESSION 29

HTML

HTML structure, headings, paragraphs, lists, links, images, tables, forms and semantic HTML. Project: personal profile webpage.

SESSION 30

CSS & Web Design

CSS, selectors, colors, fonts, borders, margins, padding, layout, Flexbox and responsive design. Project: professional responsive webpage.

SESSION 31

Python Fundamentals

Why Python, Python/Google Colab, variables, data types, input/output, operators and type conversion. Practical: simple calculator.

SESSION 32

Python Data Structures

Numbers, strings, lists, tuples, sets/frozensets, dictionaries, indexing, slicing and common methods. Practical: student database.

SESSION 33

Python Operators

Python operators and their application in programming and problem-solving.

SESSION 34

Python Decision Making

if, elif and else; decision-based programming and practical conditional problems.

SESSION 35

Python Loops

for, while, nested loops, break and continue; repetitive problem-solving through programs.

SESSION 36

Python Functions & Modular Programming

Functions, parameters, arguments, return values, scope, modules and reusable code. Project: Student Result Management Program.

SESSION 37

Introduction to AI & Machine Learning

AI and its types, Machine Learning, supervised/unsupervised learning, classification, regression, training/testing data, features and labels.

SESSION 38

Student AI/ML Mini-Project

Dataset, data preprocessing, model training, prediction, evaluation and visualization. Projects may include student performance, house price, Iris classification, spam detection and recommendation systems.

SESSION 39

AI (Continues)

Continuation of AI/ML learning, guided practice and project development based on the programme curriculum.

SESSION 40

AI (Continues)

Continuation of AI/ML learning, project refinement and hands-on work based on the programme curriculum.

Skill Cluster 4 — CREATE & LEAD

Sessions 41–48 • 12 Hours
SESSION 41

Leadership Fundamentals

Leadership, leader vs manager, leadership styles, self-awareness, responsibility, decision-making, emotional intelligence and ethical leadership.

SESSION 42

Teamwork & Collaboration

Team formation, team roles, communication, trust, conflict management, active listening, feedback and collaboration tools.

SESSION 43

Project Management for Students

Project idea, scope, objectives, deliverables, milestones, tasks, dependencies, timeline, risk and resources; Kanban, Gantt charts and Trello-style boards.

SESSION 44

Hackathon Skills

Hackathons, problem selection, team formation, ideation, problem statement, solution design, MVP, prototype, pitch and demo. Activity: 60-minute mini hackathon.

SESSION 45

Innovation & Invention

Invention, innovation, product/technology/social innovation, patent basics, intellectual property, prototyping and MVP. Activity: Problem → Solution → Prototype → Value Proposition.

SESSION 46

Student Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurial mindset, opportunity identification, customer problem, target customer, value proposition, business model, revenue model, cost, competitors and market research. Activity: Business Model Canvas.

SESSION 47

Venture Project Development

Problem/customer validation, product development, MVP, pricing, marketing, branding, digital marketing, pitch deck and financial basics. Teams develop a student venture/project.

SESSION 48

Final Innovation & Demo Day

Integrated capstone: problem, research/evidence, target users, solution, technology, prototype/MVP, innovation, business/social value, future scope and 3-minute pitch.
